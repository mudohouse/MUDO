MUDO — Modular Housing & Digital Fabrication

MUDO is a Chilean architecture and research project focused on developing affordable, sustainable, and demountable housing systems.

Founded by an independent architect and woodworker in southern Chile, MUDO combines hands-on construction experience, timber fabrication, and digital technologies.

Our Mission

To make quality housing more accessible through modular design, reusable components, and efficient construction methods.

Technology and Development

We are developing a construction system based on CNC-fabricated timber components and dry assembly techniques.

Our future open digital platform aims to enable users to:

- Design and configure modular homes in 3D.
- Customize architectural layouts.
- Generate component lists and fabrication information.
- Optimize material use and construction costs.
- Access technical documentation and collaborative design tools.

Current Status

MUDO is currently in the research, design, and prototype development stage. The digital platform is planned for progressive development and validation.

Open Development

This repository will document the development of MUDO's digital tools, technical research, and future software prototypes.

Our long-term vision is to connect architecture, digital fabrication, and open technology to help address the housing crisis in Latin America.
